3. Information We Collect

We collect information that you provide to us directly, primarily when you contact us, place an order, or message us on WhatsApp or other channels. This may include:

  • Contact details: your name, phone number, and WhatsApp number.
  • Communication content: the messages, photos, documents, or voice notes you send us.
  • Order and transaction details: products or services enquired about or purchased, and order history.
  • Technical information: basic metadata associated with your messages, such as timestamps, which is processed by the WhatsApp Business Platform.

We do not knowingly collect sensitive personal information (such as health data, religious beliefs, or biometric data) unless you voluntarily share it with us as part of a support request, and only to the extent necessary to assist you.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To respond to your enquiries and provide customer support.
  • To process, confirm, and fulfil your orders.
  • To send order updates and service-related alerts.
  • To send you offers, promotions, or product updates, where you have agreed to receive such messages.
  • To improve our products, services, and customer communication.
  • To comply with legal, tax, or regulatory obligations.
  • To detect, prevent, and address fraud, spam, or misuse of our communication channels.

We do not use your personal information for any purpose that is incompatible with the purposes listed above without informing you.

5. Communication via WhatsApp

We use the WhatsApp Business Platform, provided by Meta Platforms, Inc. ("Meta"), to communicate with our customers. When you message us on WhatsApp or receive messages from us:

  • Your messages are transmitted and processed through WhatsApp's infrastructure, in accordance with WhatsApp's own Privacy Policy and Meta's data practices.
  • We only send you business-initiated messages (such as promotions or reminders) if you have opted in to receive them, for example by messaging us first, filling a contact form, or agreeing at the point of purchase.
  • You may opt out of promotional messages at any time by replying "STOP" or contacting us directly, as described in Section 10.
  • We retain the content of conversations only for as long as necessary to provide support or fulfil an order, as described in Section 8.

6. Legal Basis for Processing

We process your personal information on the following grounds:

  • Consent: where you have given us clear consent, such as opting in to promotional messages.
  • Performance of a contract: where processing is necessary to fulfil an order or provide a service you have requested.
  • Legitimate interests: where processing helps us run and improve our business, provided this does not override your rights.
  • Legal obligation: where we are required to retain or disclose information under applicable law.

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Your Rights and Choices

You have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access: you may ask us what personal information we hold about you.
  • Correction: you may ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Deletion: you may ask us to delete your personal information, subject to legal retention requirements.
  • Withdraw consent / opt out: you may stop receiving promotional messages at any time by replying "STOP" on WhatsApp or contacting us using the details in Section 14.
  • Complaint: if you believe your data has been mishandled, you may raise a complaint with us directly, or with the relevant data protection authority in India.

We will respond to any request within a reasonable time, generally within 30 days.
